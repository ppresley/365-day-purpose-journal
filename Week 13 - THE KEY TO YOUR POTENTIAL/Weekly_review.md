# Week 13 - REVIEW the principles from this week

- What God speaks to is the source for what He creates.
- Everything has the same components as its source.
- Nothing can live without being attached to its source.
- Jesus is your Source of life.
- God prunes your life to bring you into the freedom of obedience.
- The key to fulfilling your potential is staying hooked up to God.
- Only disciples—those who are committed to abiding in Christ—will maximize their potential.
- God is glorified when you use your abilities.
