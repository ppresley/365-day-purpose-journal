# Week 14 - REVIEW the principles from this week

- When God created the earth and made the fullness of its beauty, He used the following principles:
- He purposed and decided what He wanted.
- He then decided what kind of material substance He wanted it made from.
- He then spoke to the substance or material from which He wanted it made.
- And whatever He said to what He was speaking to came out of what He spoke to—exactly what He said.
- Whatever came out of what God spoke to was made of the same material substance from which it came.
